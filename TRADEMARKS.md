## ✅ You may (no permission needed)

- **Refer to the project by name** in text — e.g. "compatible with CYPHER65
  War Room", "based on CYPHER65 War Room" — as long as it's clear you are not
  the official project (nominative fair use).
- **Fork and modify the code** under the MIT license, as long as you:
  - keep the MIT copyright notice, and
  - **rename your project** — do not call it "CYPHER65" or "CYPHER65 War Room".
- **Credit** the project ("© 2026 0xjc65eth — MIT") in your attribution.

## 🚫 You may not

- Use **CYPHER65**, **CYPHER65 WAR ROOM** or a confusingly similar name/logo
  as the name of your own product, repository, package, domain, account or
  app — especially anything derived, commercial or pretending to be official.
- Present a fork or derivative as the official CYPHER65 project (passing off).
- Register domains or usernames that mimic our name to divert traffic or
  resell (cybersquatting).
- Use the ⚡ logo or associated artwork in commercial material without
  permission.

## ✍️ Asking for permission

Commercial use of the identity (white-label, merchandise, partnerships) is
evaluated case by case — open an issue on GitHub
(`0xjc65eth/cypher65-war-room`) or reach us through the in-app support panel.

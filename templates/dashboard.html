1 · Overview

Axe Fleet is a real-time monitoring and operations platform for Bitcoin home miners. It connects to your wallet via the Parasite Pool public API and provides fleet telemetry, probability analysis, hash market intelligence, automation rules, and latency diagnostics — all from a single dashboard.

The platform works with any BTC address. Data comes from public pool endpoints, on-chain sources (Mempool, CoinGecko), and optionally from your local AxeOS/Bitaxe devices if you add them to the Axe Fleet.

i Pool data vs device data: Pool data shows what the pool sees about your workers (aggregated hashrate, shares, best diff). Device data comes directly from your miners via AxeOS API and shows temperature, efficiency, fan speed, and individual performance.

2 · Fleet View

The Fleet section displays all your miners as individual cards. Each card shows real-time status in a compact layout:

  • Status dot — green pulse (online), red (offline), orange (warning)
  • Hashrate — current processing speed in TH/s or GH/s
  • Temperature — ASIC temp in °C. Above 70°C is warning territory
  • Efficiency — J/TH ratio. Lower is better (target < 35 J/TH for Bitaxe)
  • Best Diff — highest share difficulty found by this device
  • Last Share — time since last accepted share
  • Uptime — how long the device has been running since last restart
  • Health Score — aggregate score based on temp, reject rate, uptime, and hashrate stability

The fleet summary bar shows aggregate HR, online/offline counts, average temperature, best overall diff, total power consumption, and fleet health percentage.

✦ Click on a miner card to expand device details including voltage, frequency, firmware version, and pool connection status.

3 · Device Health

Device Health is computed from multiple telemetry signals and expressed as a score from 0–100%. Components:

  • Temperature (30%) — < 60°C = healthy, 60–70°C = warn, > 70°C = critical
  • Reject Rate (20%) — < 1% = excellent, 1–3% = normal, > 3% = investigate
  • Uptime (25%) — > 24h = full score, decreases proportionally below that
  • Hashrate Stability (25%) — variance < 5% from expected = healthy

A health score below 60% triggers a WARN alert. Below 30% triggers a CRIT alert. Use the Alerts panel to configure custom thresholds for your fleet.

4 · Probability

Probability shows your estimated chance of finding a block given your hashrate, the network difficulty, and the time window. Calculated per share and per time period:

  • P(Block)/Share — chance per individual share submission
  • P(Block) 24h — cumulative probability over 24 hours
  • P(Block) 7d — cumulative probability over 7 days
  • Cumulative P — total probability since your session started
i Formula: P = 1 - exp(-(hashrate * time) / (network_difficulty * 2^32)). Results are estimates — actual luck varies.

Three scenarios are shown: conservative (95% of current HR), base (current HR), and aggressive (110% of current HR). This helps you understand the range of possible outcomes.

5 · Hash Market

The Hash Market panel aggregates live hashrate pricing from Braiins, NiceHash, MiningRigRentals, KissMyHash, and Parasite Pool. Prices are shown in BTC/TH/day and automatically scale to PH/s when appropriate.

  • Price per TH/d — how much 1 TH/s earns in 24 hours at current market rate
  • Best offer — the highest paying source right now, highlighted with a badge
  • Price Trend — 7-day chart showing price history across sources
  • Comparison — see all sources side-by-side to choose the best payout
✦ Configure API keys for Braiins, NiceHash, and MRR in Settings → Market to unlock live pricing. Without keys, only Parasite Pool public data is shown.

6 · Live Mining

Live Mining provides real-time telemetry for your fleet with automatic updates every 5 seconds. The live indicators show:

  • Live dot — green pulsing dot next to each online miner card
  • Freshness badge — shows "just now", "32s ago", etc. updates every 1 second
  • Fleet heartbeat — polls device health every 5 seconds for faster card updates
  • LIVE badge — shown in the fleet panel header when at least one device is online

If a miner stops responding, the status transitions: ONLINE → WARN (after 30s of no data) → OFFLINE (after 120s). Alerts are generated at each transition.

7 · Automations

Automations let you define rules that trigger actions when certain conditions are met. This reduces manual intervention and keeps your fleet running optimally.

Rule Components

  • Trigger — metric + operator + threshold (e.g., temperature > 70°C)
  • Device filter — apply to all devices, a specific miner, or a group
  • Action — alert, restart miner, power-cycle switch, switch pool, or notify
  • Cooldown — minimum time between repeated firings (prevents alert storms)
⚠ Safety: Power-cycle and restart actions require explicit confirmation. Batch actions on multiple devices require double confirmation. Every action is logged in the audit trail.

8 · Latency / Ping

Latency between your miner and the pool directly impacts stale share rates and efficiency. Every millisecond matters in Bitcoin mining.

  • Ping — round-trip time to the pool server in milliseconds
  • Stale share rate — shares submitted after the job expired. Above 2% indicates latency issues
  • Handshake time — how long the Stratum connection handshake takes
  • Submit roundtrip — time from share submission to acknowledgment
✦ How to reduce ping: Use wired Ethernet instead of Wi-Fi (reduces 10–50ms). Choose a pool server geographically closer to you. Avoid VPNs that route traffic through distant locations. Keep DNS resolution fast by using a local resolver.

Latency above 200ms typically increases stale shares noticeably. Below 50ms is excellent for home mining.

9 · Alerts

The Alert Engine monitors your fleet and generates events when conditions deviate from normal. Alerts are categorized by severity:

  • CRIT — immediate attention required: miner offline, 0 GH/s, temperature > 80°C
  • WARN — should investigate soon: temperature > 65°C, reject rate > 3%, stale shares > 5%
  • INFO — informational: miner came online, new best diff, pool connection restored
  • GOLD — positive events: new block found, new all-time best diff

Active alerts are shown in the Alerts panel with count badge. Click on any alert to see details. Use the Alert Center (Settings → Alerts) to configure custom thresholds and notification channels.

10 · Multi-User Isolation

Axe Fleet supports multi-tenant isolation. Each user account sees only their own wallets, miners, alerts, and automation rules. There is no data leakage between tenants.

  • Every query is filtered by tenant_id
  • Session data is encrypted and validated on each request
  • Miners from different users never appear in the same view
  • Alert and automation rules are scoped per tenant
  • Audit logs include user and tenant ID for traceability
i Multi-user mode is ideal for hosting providers, mining farms offering dashboards to clients, or teams managing shared infrastructure.

11 · Support the Project

Axe Fleet is maintained by a small independent team. Your support keeps the project alive, funds development, and helps cover infrastructure costs. Every contribution matters — no amount is too small.

You can support via:

  • Bitcoin (on-chain) — send directly to the project BTC address

Find the support panel at the bottom of the dashboard. Your contribution directly funds server costs, API integrations, and new feature development.

12 · FAQ

Why are some fields showing "—" (dash)?
Fields show dashes when data hasn't arrived yet or is not available from the source. Pool data updates every 15 seconds. Device telemetry requires the miner to be added to Axe Fleet and reachable on the network. Some metrics (efficiency, reject rate) require multiple data points before they appear.
How does the app read my miners?
When you add a miner by IP address, the app connects to the AxeOS API on the device directly (port 80 or 443). No cloud service or third party is involved. The connection stays within your local network (or Tailscale private network for remote access).
How can I improve data accuracy?
For best results: (1) connect miners via wired Ethernet, (2) use a dedicated host that stays online 24/7, (3) add miners to Axe Fleet by IP for direct telemetry, (4) keep the dashboard open in a browser tab for continuous polling.
What should I do when a miner shows 0 GH/s?
First, check if the miner is reachable (ping it). If reachable, try restarting the miner from the Fleet control panel. If unreachable, check power supply and network cable. For persistent issues, configure a power-cycle automation with a smart plug.
Why is my reject rate high?
High reject rate is usually caused by: (1) high latency to the pool server, (2) overly aggressive overclocking, (3) stale shares due to slow network, (4) pool server issues. Use the Latency panel to diagnose, and consider switching to a geographically closer pool server.
How do automations work?
Automations watch for specific conditions (e.g., temperature > 70°C) and trigger actions (alert, restart, power-cycle). Create a rule in the Alert Center, select the metric and threshold, choose the action, and set a cooldown period to prevent repeated firing. All actions require confirmation.
Can I access my dashboard remotely?
Yes. The recommended approach is Tailscale — install it on your home host and your remote devices, then access the dashboard via the Tailscale IP. This keeps all traffic encrypted and private without exposing ports to the internet. See the Remote Access panel for setup instructions.
